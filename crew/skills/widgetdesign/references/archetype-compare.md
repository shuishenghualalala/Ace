# Compare Archetype

Use when the user needs meaningful differences between bounded options.

- **Dominant structure:** aligned rows or a comparison matrix with one shared baseline.
- **P0:** winner, exception, or decisive delta.
- **P1:** the few criteria that determine the decision and the compared options.
- **P2:** full rows, evidence, caveats, and secondary trade-offs.
- **Compact:** one P0 statement: the winner or exception, optionally followed by one short decisive delta
  as its direct label; no criteria row or alternate option. **Regular:** key aligned rows. **Expanded:**
  full decision rows and evidence.
- Prefer short direct cells and consistent units. Highlight only the decisive difference, never use
  separate product cards or color alone to encode comparison.
- Narrow matrix headers under 120px do not contain a text badge such as `Recommended`; use a local
  noncolor mark plus an accessible name, or move the complete label to a shared recommendation line.
